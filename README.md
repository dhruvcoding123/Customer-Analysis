This is an AI-assisted customer early-warning system. It combines structured customer, usage, billing and satisfaction data with support-interaction
transcripts to identify customers who may need proactive attention.

The system first creates an explainable 0–100 structured risk score and prioritizes
customers. When an operator selects a customer, the AI analyzes the customer’s structured
risk signals together with recent support conversations to provide semantic signals, a
concise rationale and a recommended action.
